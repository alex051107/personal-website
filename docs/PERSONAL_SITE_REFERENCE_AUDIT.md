# Personal-site reference audit

Date: 2026-08-25

This pass looked at real personal sites rather than UI galleries. The goal was not to copy their surfaces. It was to identify how a personal homepage establishes a person, a point of view, and a body of work before it asks the visitor to understand project mechanics.

## Reference set

| Site | What the opening establishes | Useful pattern | Decision for this site |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Brittany Chiang](https://brittanychiang.com/) | Name, role, and one plain sentence about the work | The visitor knows who she is before the résumé detail appears | Adopt the direct name + work sentence; reject the long experience-led homepage because Zhenpeng's projects need the main space |
| [Lee Robinson](https://leerob.com/) | “Engineer and writer,” current work, then personal life | A short first-person bio can carry both credibility and personality | Adopt the compact first-person register; do not imitate the text-only density |
| [Maggie Appleton](https://maggieappleton.com/) | One memorable sentence about what she makes | A personal verb is stronger than a field label | Adopt “I build…” language and clear section names; keep the existing scientific images rather than a garden metaphor |
| [Josh Comeau](https://www.joshwcomeau.com/) | The work is immediately useful and written in an unmistakable voice | Friendly copy and interactive teaching can coexist with technical depth | Adopt conversational section copy; avoid decorative play inside scientific claims |
| [Paco Coursey](https://paco.me/) | A compact statement of craft and current role | Short sections named with ordinary verbs feel personal | Adopt smaller, sentence-case labels and fewer explanatory preambles |
| [Emil Kowalski](https://emilkowal.ski/) | Current role, taste, projects, then writing | Project names get one immediate line of meaning | Adopt one-line project orientation before mechanics; avoid copying the minimal white-card aesthetic |
| [Rauno Freiberg](https://rauno.me/) | A single identity sentence and a strong personal maxim | Restraint gives the signature interaction more room | Keep the 3D Harness as the one theatrical gesture and quiet the surrounding typography |
| [Anthony Fu](https://antfu.me/) | A warm greeting, current work, then created projects | First-person language makes a technically dense profile feel inhabited | Adopt a real greeting and visible name; avoid the large inventory of affiliations |
| [Steph Ango](https://stephango.com/) | A personal knowledge site organized around notes and principles | Clear titles and generous reading width make ideas feel authored | Adopt the calm reading rhythm; reject the essay-like serif voice that caused the current academic feel |
| [Lynn Fisher](https://lynnandtonic.com/) | Personality is carried by the site itself | One recognizable visual idea is enough | Preserve the interactive Harness as the signature instead of adding more unrelated effects |
| [Bruno Simon](https://bruno-simon.com/) | The interactive world is the introduction | A live object can explain capability before a case study | Keep direct manipulation, but add plain-language framing so the 3D scene never becomes a portfolio game with unclear meaning |
| [Nicky Case](https://ncase.me/) | Projects are framed as things made for people to understand | Friendly verbs and visual explanations lower the entry cost of complex ideas | Adopt the “show the idea working” principle in the Hero and stage stories; keep claims bounded and professional |

## What changes in production

1. The homepage now opens with a greeting, a real name, and a concrete description of the work.
2. Onest replaces the high-contrast display serif. One humanist sans family carries identity, headings, and reading text; Geist Mono remains limited to actual state, gate, receipt, and control readouts.
3. The desktop opening becomes a personal two-column composition with the portrait visibly paired with the introduction.
4. The sticky header carries Work, About, and Contact. The redundant dossier-style page index is removed.
5. Major headings use sentence case and direct language. Technical uppercase remains inside the interactive instrument where it encodes machine state.
6. The 3D Harness remains the signature visual, but its framing changes from “inspection plate” language to a question a visitor can understand before touching it.

## Deliberate rejection

The redesign does not copy a dark developer template, a floating-card SaaS layout, or a purely academic publication list. It also does not add a second visual gimmick beside the Harness. The personal voice and project images carry the rest of the page.
