# EvidenceOps — 12-second micro-film

## Story thesis

A relevant paper is not enough. A claim advances only when its wording, value,
unit, method, and condition remain attached to an exact source passage and a
human-reviewable record.

Suggested voice-over:

> EvidenceOps moves from papers to one exact passage, compares candidate
> support, checks every claim field, and records the source for human review.
