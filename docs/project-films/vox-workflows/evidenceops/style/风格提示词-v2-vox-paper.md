# EvidenceOps · VOX paper-collage style lock · v2

## Core medium

Create a premium 2.5D editorial paper-collage explainer, as if a VOX newsroom graphics team built a tactile evidence workflow from cut paper and filmed it from above. The work must read first as an explanation and second as an aesthetic object.

## Physical construction

- 4–10 mm separation between major paper planes
- visible scissor-cut or torn paper fibers at selected edges
- warm ivory uncoated stock, charcoal paper, recycled kraft, and sparse muted ochre
- halftone photographs, photocopy grain, dry ink, subtle offset-registration error, and restrained newsprint wear
- thin brass foil only for hinges, index tabs, and tiny state markers
- real contact shadows between paper layers; no deep glass cabinet or heavy metal machine
- matte surfaces; no glossy plastic, ray-traced reflections, lens flare, or bokeh-heavy product photography

## Palette

- paper: warm ivory and natural recycled beige
- structure: carbon charcoal, never blue-black
- active editorial signal: muted ochre
- metal accent: narrow aged-brass foil, below 10% of the frame
- no purple, cyan neon, red alert wash, or green success glow

## Editorial composition

One frame shows one question and one visible operation. Input, comparison, rejection or stop, human boundary, and durable output must be physically traceable. Preserve negative space and a strong editorial reading order. Use paper-plane parallax rather than cinematic depth for its own sake.

## EvidenceOps object grammar

- paper stack = synthetic scientific inputs
- selected page and passage window = exact inspectable evidence
- four candidate carriers = competing passages
- four inspection rows = text, value, unit, and context checks
- side tray = rejected mismatch, not failure theatre
- paper hinge = source remains attached to the candidate claim
- natural cut-out hand and pencil = human review boundary
- open archive tray = recorded review state, not approval

## Text system

Readable scene text is printed ink on a named paper carrier. It is never a subtitle, floating interface, or regenerated body copy.

Allowed labels by shot:

- shot 1: `SYNTHETIC DEMO`, `PAPER A`, `PASSAGE 03`, `TEXT`, `VALUE`, `UNIT`, `CONTEXT`
- shot 2: `PAPER A`, `PAPER B`, `PAPER C`, `PAPER D`, `EXACT SUPPORT`, `TEXT`, `VALUE`, `UNIT`, `CONTEXT`, `REJECTED`
- shot 3: `HUMAN REVIEW`, `SOURCE ATTACHED`, `RECORDED`

All other paper marks are non-readable line texture. Preserve exact spelling, case, carrier, line count, typeface, perspective, and contrast. In v2 shot 3, `SOURCE ATTACHED` is intentionally set on two fixed lines inside one black hinge plate.

## Motion grammar

- one dominant movement at a time; no more than one third of visible objects move simultaneously
- camera translation stays below 5% of frame width per 4-second clip
- camera rotation stays below 1°; no orbit and no sudden zoom
- paper cards move as rigid cut-outs along visible rails or hinges
- begin with 0.30–0.40 seconds of visual stability and end with a 0.30–0.40 second edit handle
- use foreground paper occlusion for transitions; never use liquid morph, particle dissolve, flash, or generic AI warp

## Avoid

Photorealistic museum miniature, steampunk apparatus, heavy metal-and-glass cabinet, generic AI brain, robot mascot, neural sphere, glowing lines, particle cloud, SaaS dashboard, fake scientific content, autonomous approval, clinical decision, illegible generated prose, extra objects, duplicated pages, changing labels, floating captions, logo, or watermark.
