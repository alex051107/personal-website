# Portfolio archival-machine visual system

> Superseded for new generations. This v1 file produced a photorealistic miniature rather than the requested VOX paper-collage medium. Use `风格提示词-v2-vox-paper.md` after the v2 trial receives explicit approval.

Create a 16:9 reference and keyframe system for an AI-for-Science portfolio. The visual language is a tactile editorial miniature set, not the default red Vox news collage.

## Mood

Calm, exact, scientific, human-readable, museum-exhibit quality. A viewer should understand the workflow operation before noticing the visual effects.

## Materials

- warm ivory paper with visible fibers and restrained archival wear
- carbon-black work surface and enclosures
- oxidized brass rails, clips, hinges, gates, and labels
- muted ochre as the EvidenceOps active signal
- soft raking side light, shallow depth of field, realistic contact shadows
- layered paper depth and foreground occlusion; never flat infographic cards

## Composition

Use one active event per frame. Keep the input, current operation, rejected route or stop, and durable output physically connected. Previous state remains visible as evidence. Key labels stay inside the central 70% safe area.

## EvidenceOps object grammar

- papers and passage cards are the inputs
- a brass comparison rail aligns candidates
- exact-support checks are physical inspection rows
- a mismatch exits through a visible side branch
- source and proposed claim remain attached by a paper hinge
- the human reviewer is represented only by a natural hand and pencil
- the durable output is a source-linked review record

## Scene text

Only short physical labels are allowed. Use `SYNTHETIC DEMO`, `PAPER A`, `PAPER B`, `PAPER C`, `PAPER D`, `PASSAGE 03`, `TEXT`, `VALUE`, `UNIT`, `CONTEXT`, `EXACT SUPPORT`, `REJECTED`, `HUMAN REVIEW`, `SOURCE ATTACHED`, and `RECORDED` when mapped by the shot. No real title, author, DOI, journal, institution, date, signature, metric, or biomedical conclusion. Narration subtitles are added in post and must not appear in generated frames.

## Typography

Condensed editorial sans for primary titles; bold uppercase simple sans for machine and status labels; medium condensed sans for annotations. Ink-black text on ivory paper; warm-white text on carbon-black carriers. No red card, red text, or mustard card is used in this project.

## Motion grammar

One concept + one visible operation + one continuous camera move per clip. EvidenceOps uses overhead push, lateral rail trace, and dolly-back. Animate paper, rails, shutters, hands, and blank mechanisms around printed text. Printed text and numbers never morph, count, flicker, mirror, duplicate, disappear, or move to another carrier.

## Avoid

No robot, glowing brain, blue or purple neon, generic node sphere, glossy SaaS UI, plastic CG, lens flare, random equations, decorative gears without workflow meaning, fake citations, invented scientific results, floating text, subtitle bars, watermarks, brand marks, or illegible micro-copy.
