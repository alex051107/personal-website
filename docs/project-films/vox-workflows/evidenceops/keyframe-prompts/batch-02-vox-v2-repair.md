Avoid photorealistic museum miniature, steampunk apparatus, deep metal-and-glass cabinet, glossy plastic CGI, ray-traced reflections, blue-black AI palette, cyan or purple neon, red alert wash, green success glow, robot, AI brain, neural sphere, particles, floating interface, fake scientific claims, logo, or watermark.
