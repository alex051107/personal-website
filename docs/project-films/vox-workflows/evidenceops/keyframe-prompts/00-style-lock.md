# EvidenceOps GPT Image style lock

- **Use case:** scientific-educational + stylized-concept
- **Asset:** 16:9 website micro-film keyframe
- **Visual system:** tactile editorial miniature set; warm ivory paper, carbon-black work surface, oxidized brass mechanics, muted ochre active signal
- **Lighting:** soft raking side light, realistic contact shadows, shallow depth of field
- **Depth:** foreground occlusion, separated paper layers, real miniature scale; never a flat flowchart or glossy application screen
- **Motion feasibility:** every keyframe must visibly contain the rail, hinge, gate, drawer, or paper carrier that will move in the matching four-second shot
- **Text ownership:** scene text is printed on physical paper or machine carriers; narration subtitles are added in post
- **Truth boundary:** synthetic labels only; no real paper title, author, DOI, institution, date, signature, metric, or biomedical conclusion
- **Palette exclusion:** no red card, red accent, blue/purple neon, or green success glow
- **Rendering exclusion:** no robot, glowing brain, generic network sphere, plastic CG, lens flare, floating UI, subtitle bar, logo, or watermark
