## SHOT-001 · Papers become one exact passage

- **Source span:** `input/script.md:L3`
- **Exact narration:** “EvidenceOps moves from papers to one exact passage.”
- **Estimated narration:** 3.2 seconds
- **Duration:** base 4 seconds; recommended 4 seconds; mapped directly to one visual claim
- **Concept:** relevance is narrowed to an inspectable source span
- **Visual event:** four synthetic paper carriers enter an archive tray; `PAPER A` advances while its paragraph opens into one mounted passage with four attached field tabs
- **Background:** carbon-black archive table, inactive comparison rail continuing out of frame, soft warm falloff
- **Foreground:** one out-of-focus brass clip crosses the lower-left edge to establish miniature depth
- **Subject:** the same warm-ivory paper carrier transforms from a page into a close passage mount without losing its `SYNTHETIC DEMO` identity
- **Focus:** starts on the incoming pages and finishes on `PASSAGE 03`; one rack focus is allowed at 3.0 seconds
- **Camera:** overhead push; a single slow push forward with a slight downward tilt, no cut and no orbit
- **Static keyframe:** four stacked paper carriers are already seated at the intake; `PAPER A` is foremost and the passage mount is visible deeper on the same rail
- **Forward animation:** `PAPER A` advances; the page layers separate; the exact passage rises on the same carrier; `TEXT`, `VALUE`, `UNIT`, and `CONTEXT` tabs unfold but their text never changes
- **Scene text:** `SYNTHETIC DEMO`, `PAPER A`, `PASSAGE 03`, `TEXT`, `VALUE`, `UNIT`, `CONTEXT`
- **SFX:** paper feed, one brass rail click, four restrained tab clicks
- **Avoid:** fake paper title, author, DOI, real metric, floating UI, subtitle bar, robot, neon, multiple camera moves, text generation during animation

## SHOT-002 · Candidates pass or fail exact support

- **Source span:** `input/script.md:L5`
- **Exact narration:** “It compares candidate support and checks every claim field.”
- **Estimated narration:** 3.6 seconds
- **Duration:** base 4 seconds; recommended 4 seconds; one comparison event with staggered field checks
- **Concept:** topical relevance is insufficient; a candidate must support the exact claim fields
- **Visual event:** four passage carriers align on one brass rail; the chosen carrier opens into an `EXACT SUPPORT` inspection plate while one mismatch exits through the visible `REJECTED` branch
- **Background:** the first shot’s archive table continues laterally; the human-review desk remains distant and inactive
- **Foreground:** an out-of-focus brass index wheel briefly occludes the lower-right edge during the trace
- **Subject:** the comparison rail and the source-attached inspection plate; rejected evidence remains visible instead of disappearing
- **Focus:** fixed on the active inspection plate; no rack focus
- **Camera:** lateral rail trace; one continuous left-to-right truck matching the candidate motion
- **Static keyframe:** `PAPER A–D` are equally visible on separate carriers; the inspection plate and rejection side branch already exist but are inactive
- **Forward animation:** the index marker moves once to the selected carrier; the five blank mechanical indicators resolve around immutable printed labels; the mismatching carrier moves to `REJECTED`; the accepted source stays attached
- **Scene text:** `PAPER A`, `PAPER B`, `PAPER C`, `PAPER D`, `TEXT`, `VALUE`, `UNIT`, `CONTEXT`, `EXACT SUPPORT`, `REJECTED`
- **SFX:** index-wheel ticks, five muted inspection clicks, one low side-tray stop

## SHOT-003 · A human receives a source-linked record

- **Source span:** `input/script.md:L7`
- **Exact narration:** “Then it records the source for human review.”
- **Estimated narration:** 3.3 seconds
- **Duration:** base 4 seconds; recommended 4 seconds; direct handoff and loop return
- **Concept:** the system records reviewable evidence but does not release or approve the claim
- **Visual event:** the source-attached claim reaches a human desk; a pencil verifies the attachment; the hinged pair closes into one record and enters the archive while the next paper stack returns
- **Background:** the comparison rail terminates at a carbon-black record drawer; the opening archive tray is visible behind it for the loop
- **Foreground:** a softly blurred natural hand and pencil enter from the right, never covering the source carrier
- **Subject:** one hinged source/claim pair becoming a durable review record
- **Focus:** begins on `HUMAN REVIEW`, racks once to `RECORDED` after the hand withdraws
- **Camera:** dolly-back; one calm retreat that reveals the review boundary, record drawer, and loop-return paper stack
- **Static keyframe:** the hinged source/claim pair is stopped before the human desk; the record drawer is open; no approval control exists
- **Forward animation:** the pencil touches the source attachment once; the hand withdraws; the pair folds into one record; the record enters the drawer; a new synthetic paper stack settles in the opening orientation
- **Scene text:** `HUMAN REVIEW`, `SOURCE ATTACHED`, `RECORDED`
- **SFX:** pencil touch, paper hinge fold, drawer slide, restrained stamp, final paper rustle
- **Avoid:** autonomous approval, signature, date, reviewer name, face reveal, medical conclusion, glowing success state, text mutation, visible jump at the loop boundary

## Manual semantic review checklist

- Narration order is complete and source spans are unique.
- Each shot contains one concept, one visible operation, and one continuous camera move.
- Input, exact-support gate, rejected branch, human boundary, and durable record are visually explicit.
- Scene text is short, physical, synthetic, and mapped to a carrier.
- No shot claims scientific correctness, autonomous release, or production deployment.
- The three camera families do not repeat.
