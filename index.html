Why this work

The useful system starts where the model response ends.

Models can retrieve, draft, and suggest. A usable application must also preserve source context and data meaning, enforce permissions and state transitions, record failures, and evaluate results at the correct unit of evidence. My projects address those requirements across scientific and stateful workflows.

EvidenceOps

Jun 2026—Present

EvidenceOps keeps each claim tied to the passage, value, unit, and experimental context that support it.

I built the retrieval and review workflow so a relevant paper is not treated as sufficient evidence when the cited passage does not match. The system returns “unsupported” instead.

Research method note · UNC Miao Lab

HSP90 / LiGaMD

Jun 2025—Present

The HSP90 workflow turns three LiGaMD replicas into one system-level record before model evaluation.

At UNC’s Miao Lab, I built the feature and evaluation path shown here: endpoint checks, 512 saved frames per replica, replica-level Dynamic10 summaries, one row per system, and fold-local grouped evaluation.

  1. 3 replicas
  2. 1 system row
  3. Ligand holdout

Frozen registryN31 / 27

31 systems · 27 exact-ligand groups · no model selected

Current scope Experimental assay-derived pKoff; no physical koff or broad-generalization claim.

About

Biology and Computer Science at UNC Chapel Hill.

I am an undergraduate at UNC Chapel Hill studying Biology and Computer Science. I build backend AI applications and research software for evidence, stateful workflows, and molecular data.

At UNC’s Miao Lab, I analyze LiGaMD trajectories and evaluate models against experimental pKoff measurements. I also contribute to Dynamics Atlas, a research collaboration that examines whether different molecular datasets are comparable enough to support the same analysis.

I use AI-assisted coding during implementation. For the components described here as my work, I define the problem, choose the architecture, review failures, and interpret the recorded results; lab and collaboration context is identified separately.
